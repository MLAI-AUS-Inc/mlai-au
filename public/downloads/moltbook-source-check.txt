Pages are PDF file pages, not printed numbering.
Scope: 9,838 authors with at least five posts qualify for timing analysis;
the full 55,932-author population is not the classification denominator.
Unresolved: limitations report 0% extended-dataset LLM analysis while methods

Completed check 3: changing the label threshold changes the percentage
Source version and locators: Jiang et al., arXiv 2602.10127v1,
2 February 2026, LLM-Driven Labeling Pipeline, Table 1, General Toxicity Level,
and Harmfulness Over Time.
https://arxiv.org/html/2602.10127v1
Reported counts: 44,411 collected; 44,376 annotated; 381 human-labelled comparison
posts. Difference: 35 excluded posts, not 35 safe posts.
Table 1 labels: Safe 32,399; Edgy 3,733; Toxic 4,634; Manipulative 2,977;
Malicious 633. They sum to 44,376 annotated posts.
Our arithmetic: all non-safe = 11,977 / 44,376 = 26.99%, not the prose's 27.05%.
Our arithmetic: levels 2-4 = 8,244 / 44,376 = 18.58%. This applies the hourly
analysis's narrower label definition to the overall counts; the paper does not
separately report this overall percentage. Edgy is excluded from this numerator.
Design decision: state the threshold and denominator. Source-assigned labels
are not a validated deployment risk score, causal harm estimate or independent
validation of a new moderation system.
