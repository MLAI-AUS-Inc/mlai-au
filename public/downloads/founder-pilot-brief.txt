COMPLETED EXAMPLE

BUYER, USER AND DECISION
Fictional buyer: an operations lead. User: one weekly-report operator. Budget approver: the business owner. Test whether a review-only project summary is useful enough to justify a separate real-data assessment; do not promise time savings or a production rollout.

WORKFLOW AND BASELINE
One weekly summary covering two projects and up to 12 supplied updates. Assume 45 minutes of manual preparation per summary for planning only; no baseline has been measured. Before judging value, the operator must time matched manual and pilot cases, including checking and correction, and record sample size and case difficulty.

DELIVERABLE AND INPUT BOUNDARY
One test workspace, one operator, synthetic JSON updates with workspaceId, projectId, updateId and text; no live integration. Deliver a review-only draft, source IDs beside each factual claim, visible conflicts/missing information, source/configuration, evaluation record and operator notes. Source-ID presence alone does not establish that a claim is supported.

EXCLUDED ACTIONS
No customer messages, external publication, status changes, other workspaces or real personal/customer data. The operator manually decides what to publish outside the pilot. Remove send/write credentials and verify the boundary; prompt instructions alone are not an access control.

ACCEPTANCE AND OWNER
Propose the eight cases below as a minimum starting set, all currently UNRUN. The founder implements and records results; the operations lead must agree expected outcomes and review failures; the owner decides acceptance. These roles are fictional and no reviewer is assigned. Passing eight cases would not establish general reliability or operating approval.

DEPENDENCIES AND SCHEDULE
Four proposed weeks: scope/fixture in week 1, review-only draft in week 2, failure testing in week 3, handover in week 4. Buyer supplies an approved synthetic fixture and two review slots before work starts. Dates, access and availability are unconfirmed. Preserve the founder's existing product release in week 2 and customer demonstration in week 4.

FEE AND COLLECTION ASSUMPTIONS
Illustrative A$1,800 fee excluding GST, not a quote or agreed rate. Propose A$900 invoiced after agreed scope/start authorisation, assumed collected in week 2, and A$900 after acceptance/handover, optimistically collected in week 4. Payment terms and calendar dates remain unagreed. Test late or absent receipts; acceptance and an invoice are not cash.

COSTS, PAYER AND USAGE STOP
Founder-paid hypothetical outlays: week 1 A$100 test infrastructure plus A$250 external-review allowance; weeks 2 and 3 A$100 provider usage each; week 4 A$150 external handover-review allowance. Total A$700 excluding GST. These are allowances, not vendor quotes or completed reviews. Paid external review is separate from the founder's own review hours. Propose a cumulative A$200 provider-usage cap; show a hard stop before an over-budget call. No live running-cost commitment is included; later cash flows are outside the worksheet.

CHANGES AND STOP CONDITIONS
A new workspace, real data or send/write capability requires a new scope, risk review, estimate and explicit approval. Stop on a permission breach, unsupported factual claim, lost input or absent budget control. Keep original updates available and use the manual report. A failed case is a revise/stop decision, not an automatic new feature allowance.

OWNERSHIP AND REUSE
Propose delivery of permitted source, configuration and operator notes to the buyer. Background code, third-party licences, ownership/licensing and reuse rights require an explicit agreement and appropriate advice. Do not assume client-funded work can become startup IP or a public portfolio. No reuse permission has been granted in this example.

HANDOVER AND SUPPORT BOUNDARY
Reserve four founder hours in week 4 for reproduction, disabling/fallback practice, documentation and bounded support. Propose two additional one-hour support slots in week 5 for reproducible defects within agreed scope, not feature changes or ongoing operation. No later hours are reserved yet. Owner acceptance, actual slot dates and support terms remain unconfirmed; legal obligations are not defined by this worksheet.

CURRENT PLANNING DECISION
NOT READY TO COMMIT. The original plan needs 28 hours within four weeks but has only 24; weeks 1 and 2 are overloaded. Week-5 support needs two more hours with none reserved. Acceptance cases are unrun and terms unresolved. Reduce/resequence scope or negotiate a feasible schedule, reserve support and review cash timing before deciding to accept, defer or decline.
