HOW TO USE YOUR OWN RECORD
Set a signal before the test. Record whether it ran, what you observed and what remains unknown. Include dates, permission, sample, time period and any independent verification in the evidence field.
A self-reported observation is not independent verification. An unanswered invitation is not a refusal. A missed signal cannot be fixed by changing the threshold after the test.
The article's 90-day sequence is a proposed plan, not a proven timetable or permission to trade, hire, process personal data or spend money.
