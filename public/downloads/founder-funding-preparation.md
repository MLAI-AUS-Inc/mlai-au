### Fictional example (AUD), not a recommended raise

Assume $30,000 available, $6,000 setup and $12,000 monthly costs.
No revenue or new investment is assumed.

| Scenario | Cost | Gap against available cash |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Three months | $42,000 | $12,000 |
| Five months | $66,000 | $36,000 |

The two-month delay adds $24,000. This example omits tax, financing costs and
other obligations, and does not model dates within each month. A real plan must
include everything applicable. Neither gap automatically determines a raise
amount or shows the milestone is worth pursuing. Expected investment is not
cash in hand. Do not commit to costs on the assumption that funding will arrive.

## Completed fictional cost breakdown — 29 October 2026

The existing A$12,000/month scenario comprises A$8,000 team payments, A$1,500 inference/hosting, A$1,000 evaluation/security, A$500 customer research and A$1,000 contingency. A$6,000 one-off setup comprises A$4,000 implementation and A$2,000 data/access preparation. These are invented allowances, not quotes; applicable tax and other obligations remain to be added.

Milestone: one bounded review-only pilot, agreed acceptance cases, retained failures, and a recorded continuation decision. Owner: fictional founder for cash; delivery owner and buyer reviewer still need agreement. Stop for missing permission/reviewer or unresolved funding. No paid adoption is assumed.

| Scenario | Month 1 | Month 2 | Month 3 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| A$30,000 opening, setup in month1, no receipts | A$12,000 | A$0 | -A$12,000 |
| Hypothetical A$6,000 receipt in month2 | A$12,000 | A$6,000 | -A$6,000 |
| Same receipt delayed to month4 | A$12,000 | A$0 | -A$12,000 |

The delay increases the three-month gap by A$6,000 relative to receipt on time. These are month-end balances, not daily liquidity or an available overdraft; the receipt is not a sale or forecast. The original two-extra-month scenario remains separate and adds A$24,000 of spending.
