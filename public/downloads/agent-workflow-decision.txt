THREE DESIGNS — SAME MONTHLY WORKLOAD
Baseline: 50 requests x 8 active minutes = 400 minutes. Case time includes checking, correction and fallback; extra oversight is separate, not the same review counted twice. A$60/hour values internal capacity, not avoidable payroll.

Fixed automation
M-104 handling: Required-field rules can flag the missing unit and route M-104 to a person. Do not guess which access note applies.
Error consequence: A weak rule may accept an incomplete request. A scheduler checks completeness; missed details and recovery costs are not measured.
Failure and maintenance owner: Office manager owns the rule list, checks rejected requests and fixes changed field requirements.
Planned monthly work: 50 x 6 = 300 case minutes + 60 extra oversight minutes. Potential capacity release: 40 minutes.
Setup: A$150 external cash + 3 internal hours (A$180) = A$330 combined economic setup.
Monthly cash: subscription A$10, usage A$0, external support A$10, extra labour A$0; total A$20.
Monthly cash change: −A$20. No positive cash payback.
Decision: Prepare an improved-form and manual-queue trial first; no live trial is approved.

AI-assisted workflow
M-104 handling: A fixed extraction-and-review sequence could draft the question from the request and both notes. Conflicting access information still needs a person.
Error consequence: A draft may omit a conflict or invent a booking. Office staff must inspect the original and every proposed commitment; sending remains unavailable.
Failure and maintenance owner: Office manager reviews drafts; the implementer maintains extraction versions, source references and the failure queue.
Planned monthly work: 50 x 5 = 250 case minutes + 100 extra oversight minutes. Potential capacity release: 50 minutes.
Setup: A$400 external cash + 6 internal hours (A$360) = A$760 combined economic setup.
Monthly cash: subscription A$20, usage A$10, external support A$20, extra labour A$0; total A$50.
Monthly cash change: −A$50. No positive cash payback.
Decision: Consider only if the improved form leaves a material free-text burden. The planned 50-minute release is not an observed gain.

Bounded agent
M-104 handling: Choosing which permitted note to read cannot establish which conflicting note is current. M-104 does not demonstrate a need for dynamic tool choice.
Error consequence: Wrong lookups, looping and cross-record access add failure paths. Read-only permissions, limits and review still require verification.
Failure and maintenance owner: Office manager handles unresolved work and can request a pause; the implementer owns access revocation, limit enforcement and incident diagnosis.
Planned monthly work: 50 x 4 = 200 case minutes + 180 extra oversight minutes. Potential capacity release: 20 minutes.
Setup: A$900 external cash + 12 internal hours (A$720) = A$1,620 combined economic setup.
Monthly cash: subscription A$20, usage A$30, external support A$40, extra labour A$0; total A$90.
Monthly cash change: −A$90. No positive cash payback.
Decision: No agent justified yet. Require evidence of useful variable decisions that the simpler workflow cannot handle, within tested boundaries.
Sensitivity: seven assisted case minutes rather than five gives 50 x 7 + 100 = 450 minutes, or −50 net minutes. It uses more time than the baseline; this is not an observed outcome.
Actual prices, support terms, error losses, revenue, taxes, financing and workload variability are not established. Obtain quotes and consistent cost/tax treatment; these exclusions prevent a complete ROI claim.
