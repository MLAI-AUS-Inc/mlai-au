Extract supplier-invoice fields
Monthly workload and rework: 40 cases x 8 minutes = 320 baseline minutes; 6 cases need correction within that total.
Planned assisted work: 40 x 4 = 160 case minutes, plus 60 additional oversight minutes. Potential capacity release: 100 minutes/month.
Data availability: Invented invoice samples can be used for a demonstration. Live supplier records have not been approved for connection; the accounts lead must confirm permission and source access.
Human oversight: Accounts lead checks every proposed field against the original. No posting to accounts, bank-detail changes or payments are in scope.
Cost of error: A wrong total, duplicate or misread credit creates reconciliation work. Payment must remain unavailable. The cost and frequency of escaped errors are unknown, not zero.
Setup cash: A$300; internal setup: 4 hours x A$60 = A$240; combined setup economic value: A$540.
Incremental monthly cash: subscription A$15, usage A$5, external support A$10, extra labour cash A$0; total A$30.
Monthly cash change with no avoidable payroll: −A$30. No positive cash payback under these assumptions. Capacity is not profit.
Non-AI alternative: Compare manual entry, supplier CSV exports and existing accounting rules before a model.
Decision: Investigate invoice extraction. Prepare a synthetic demonstration brief with the accounts lead, then check document-level quality and review effort. This is not permission for a live pilot or a purchase.

Draft routine customer replies
Monthly workload and rework: 60 cases x 5 minutes = 300 baseline minutes; 9 cases need correction within that total.
Planned assisted work: 60 x 3 = 180 case minutes, plus 90 additional oversight minutes. Potential capacity release: 30 minutes/month.
Data availability: The fictional returns policy has conflicting versions. No approved answer source is ready; reviewer time has not been committed.
Human oversight: The operations lead must approve the policy and assign a reviewer. Any future system would draft only; sending and commitments remain separate.
Cost of error: An unsupported refund, price or delivery promise can create a customer dispute. Consequential losses are unpriced; a fluent answer is not a control.
Setup cash: A$150; internal setup: 3 hours x A$60 = A$180; combined setup economic value: A$330.
Incremental monthly cash: subscription A$15, usage A$5, external support A$5, extra labour cash A$0; total A$25.
Monthly cash change with no avoidable payroll: −A$25. No positive cash payback under these assumptions. Capacity is not profit.
Non-AI alternative: Repair the policy and saved replies first.
Decision: Fix the source first. Do not select this AI trial while policy versions conflict or reviewer capacity is unknown, even if the arithmetic appears favourable.

Suggest compatible catalogue items
Monthly workload and rework: 120 cases x 3 minutes = 360 baseline minutes; 12 cases need correction within that total.
Planned assisted work: 120 x 2 = 240 case minutes, plus 60 additional oversight minutes. Potential capacity release: 60 minutes/month.
Data availability: The fictional catalogue has approved compatibility fields and a catalogue owner. Customer profiling data is unnecessary for the initial comparison.
Human oversight: Staff confirm suitability and stock before recommending an item. A ranking does not approve a sale or determine customer eligibility.
Cost of error: Wrong compatibility can cause returns, replacement work and trust damage. These losses are not measured or priced into this planning example.
Setup cash: A$300; internal setup: 6 hours x A$60 = A$360; combined setup economic value: A$660.
Incremental monthly cash: subscription A$10, usage A$5, external support A$10, extra labour cash A$0; total A$25.
Monthly cash change with no avoidable payroll: −A$25. No positive cash payback under these assumptions. Capacity is not profit.
Non-AI alternative: An exact compatibility filter using the existing catalogue is the first comparison.
Decision: Try the catalogue filter first. The highest task volume is not a reason to add AI. Establish what the simpler filter cannot do before considering generated recommendations.
