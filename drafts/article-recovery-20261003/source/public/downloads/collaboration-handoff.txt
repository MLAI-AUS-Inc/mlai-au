COMPLETED FICTIONAL HANDOFF — NOT APPROVED FOR LIVE USE

1. Workflow and business consequence of a missed handoff:
Accepted quote to scheduling in a fictional 12-person service business. Missed requests create chasing and rework; no lost-revenue amount is assumed.

2. Trigger / current source of truth / unique record ID:
Sales marks a quote ready in the existing approved-quote register. Q-104 plus approved version identifies the handoff. Chat and the scheduling board link to that record; neither becomes the approval authority.

3. Required information and who checks it:
Sales checks the approved scope, approval evidence, version and permitted contact reference. The scheduler checks completeness before accepting. These are fictional placeholders, not customer records.

4. Sending owner / receiving owner / backup owner:
Sales lead sends; duty scheduler accepts; operations manager is the authorised backup and decision owner. A notification or read receipt does not transfer responsibility.

5. Definition of ready / explicit acceptance by receiver:
Complete approved version is ready. The receiver records acceptance of that exact version. Missing approval or an unavailable authorised receiver stays visibly unresolved; no service date is promised.

6. Allowed changes / actions requiring human approval:
An alert may link to the task. Price, scope, acceptance and customer commitments require the responsible person's decision. A later Q-104 version goes back for review instead of silently replacing accepted work.

7. Missing information, duplicate and absence handling:
Missing approval: HOLD. Same ID/version repeated: find the existing handoff. Changed version: review the difference. Absent scheduler: named authorised backup, otherwise manual queue. These rules have not been implemented or tested.

8. Access groups / offboarding / export and recovery owner:
Sales may mark ready; scheduling may read permitted scope and accept; the operations manager manages the backup and unresolved queue. Unrelated customer records stay excluded. The administrator must demonstrate account removal and recovery before a live trial.

9. Baseline period, case count, active minutes and rework:
Invented baseline week: 40 handoffs at 12 active minutes each = 480 minutes. Six required chasing/correction, already included in that time. No actual baseline was collected.

10. Pilot period, comparable cases and exception log:
Proposed next comparable 40 cases: target 7 active minutes each, including case review/rework, plus 80 minutes of additional weekly oversight. Cases H-01 to H-06 below are NOT RUN. Keep missed requests and difficult cases; do not select only easy transfers.

11. Setup cost / ongoing cost / cash spending actually avoidable:
Process-only planning assumption: 3 internal setup hours; existing subscriptions unchanged if their controls are adequate. At an invented A$50/hour, setup capacity is valued at A$150, not an extra cash invoice. No payroll reduction or extra contribution is evidenced.

12. Stop conditions / manual fallback / decision owner and date:
Stop on a lost request, unauthorised access or unapproved commitment. Retain the approved-quote register and a reconciled manual queue. Operations manager decides after reviewing exceptions and actual effort; review date is not yet set. NOT APPROVED for live use.
